    近日，实验室研究成果 SHAPE: Spatial Hyperbolic-Embedding Augmented Pretraining for
    Estimated Time of Arrival on Obfuscated Trajectories 被ICDM
    2026接收。预计到达时间（ETA）是网约车、外卖配送、物流调度等位置服务的核心算法能力，其精度高度依赖高质量、高精度的 GPS
    轨迹数据。该论文针对轨迹隐私混淆场景下的ETA预测这一现实难题，提出了一种基于空间双曲嵌入增强预训练的全新方法。

  en: The estimated arrival time (ETA) is a core algorithmic capability for
    location-based services such as ride-hailing, food delivery, and logistics
    scheduling. Its accuracy is highly dependent on high-quality and
    high-precision GPS trajectory data. This paper addresses the practical
    challenge of predicting the estimated arrival time (ETA) in scenarios where
    trajectory privacy is obscured and proposes a novel method based on spatial
    hyperbolic embedding augmented pre-training.
