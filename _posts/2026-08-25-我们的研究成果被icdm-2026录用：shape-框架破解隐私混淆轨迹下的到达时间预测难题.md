预计到达时间（ETA）是网约车、外卖配送、物流调度等位置服务的核心算法能力，其精度高度依赖高质量、高精度的 GPS 轨迹数据。然而，随着隐私法规趋严和用户安全意识的提升，轨迹数据在采集与流转中普遍经过**轨迹混淆（Trajectory Obfuscation）**处理——轨迹点被随机扰动、降频采样甚至局部删除。这导致下游 ETA 模型面对的空间信息稀疏、偏移、失真，预测精度显著下降，成为制约"隐私合规与智能出行并行"的关键瓶颈。

我们观察到，轨迹背后路网结构的层级性与树状特性（区域—街道—路段—车道层层嵌套）在欧几里得空间中难以被高效刻画。为此，我们引入双曲几何，构建了一套完整的预训练-微调范式：

1. **空间双曲嵌入（Spatial Hyperbolic Embedding）**：将路网与轨迹点映射至双曲空间（庞加莱球模型），借助其随半径指数增长的几何容量，以低维度充分承载深层空间层级信息；
2. **增广预训练（Augmented Pretraining）**：在预训练阶段设计多种自监督增广策略，模拟轨迹混淆带来的扰动、缺失与偏移，使编码器在"信息残缺"的输入上依然学到稳健的空间表征；
3. **轻量微调适配**：预训练所得空间编码器可即插即用地接入主流 ETA 模型，兼容现有部署方式，成本低、通用性强。

该工作首次从几何结构层面回答了"空间信息被模糊化后如何重建其表达能力"这一基础问题，为隐私计算与时空智能的交叉研究提供了可复用的方法论。下一步，我们将进一步探索双曲表示在其他隐私受限时空任务（如路径恢复、城市路网表征学习）中的应用。{: .lang-zh}

The estimated arrival time (ETA) is a core algorithmic capability for location-based services such as ride-hailing, food delivery, and logistics scheduling. Its accuracy is highly dependent on high-quality and high-precision GPS trajectory data. However, with stricter privacy regulations and increased user security awareness, trajectory data is generally processed through trajectory obfuscation during collection and transmission - trajectory points are randomly perturbed, downsampled, or locally deleted. This results in sparse, shifted, and distorted spatial information for downstream ETA models, significantly reducing their prediction accuracy and becoming a key bottleneck hindering the "parallelism of privacy compliance and intelligent mobility".

We observe that the hierarchical and tree-like characteristics of the road network structure behind the trajectories (region - street - road segment - lane layer-by-layer nesting) are difficult to be efficiently captured in Euclidean space. Therefore, we introduce hyperbolic geometry and construct a complete pre-training - fine-tuning framework:

1. **Spatial Hyperbolic Embedding**: Map the road network and trajectory points to hyperbolic space (Poincaré sphere model), leveraging its exponentially growing geometric capacity to fully carry deep spatial hierarchical information in a low-dimensional manner;
2. **Augmented Pretraining**: Design various self-supervised augmentation strategies during the pre-training stage to simulate the perturbations, omissions, and shifts brought by trajectory obfuscation, enabling the encoder to still learn robust spatial representations from "incomplete" input information;
3. **Lightweight Fine-tuning Adaptation**: The spatial encoder obtained from pre-training can be directly plugged into mainstream ETA models, compatible with existing deployment methods, with low cost and strong universality.

This work first answers the fundamental question of "how to reconstruct the expression ability of spatial information after it is blurred" from the geometric structure perspective, providing a reusable methodology for the cross-disciplinary research of privacy computing and spatiotemporal intelligence. In the next step, we will further explore the application of hyperbolic representation in other privacy-constrained spatiotemporal tasks (such as path recovery, urban road network representation learning).{: .lang-en}
