  zh: >-
    近日，实验室研究成果 “DSTA4D: Content-aware Adaptive Decoupling for 4D Point Cloud
    Video Understanding” 被国际期刊 Pattern Recognition 接收。该研究聚焦 4D
    点云视频理解，旨在提升智能体对动态三维环境的感知与建模能力。


    4D 点云同时包含三维空间结构和连续时间变化信息，是自动驾驶、机器人感知和动态场景理解等任务的重要数据形式。然而，长序列点云具有复杂的空间结构与时间变化特征，不同场景的时空信息分布也存在明显差异，给高效、统一的时空建模带来了挑战。


    针对这一问题，该研究提出 DSTA4D，通过内容感知的自适应机制对空间信息和时间信息进行解耦与动态建模。模型能够根据输入点云序列自身的时空特征，灵活调整空间与时间信息的处理方式，使计算过程更加关注当前场景中的关键特征。


    此外，研究设计了轻量级的 Dynamic Spatio-Temporal Adapter（DST-Adapter），通过动态调节不同特征路径之间的信息融合，实现针对不同输入内容的自适应时空特征增强。该方法为 4D 点云视频理解提供了一种更加灵活、高效的建模范式。
  en: >-
    Recently, our research work “DSTA4D: Content-aware Adaptive Decoupling for
    4D Point Cloud Video Understanding” was accepted by the international
    journal Pattern Recognition. The study focuses on 4D point cloud video
    understanding, with the goal of improving intelligent agents’ perception and
    modeling capabilities in dynamic 3D environments.


    4D point clouds capture both 3D spatial structures and temporal dynamics, making them an important data modality for applications such as autonomous driving, robotic perception, and dynamic scene understanding. However, long-sequence point clouds exhibit complex spatial structures and temporal variations, while different scenarios may contain substantially different spatio-temporal patterns, making efficient and unified modeling challenging.


    To address this issue, the study proposes DSTA4D, which decouples and dynamically models spatial and temporal information through a content-aware adaptive mechanism. The model can adjust how spatial and temporal features are processed according to the characteristics of each input sequence, allowing computation to focus more effectively on the most informative aspects of the scene.


    In addition, the study introduces a lightweight Dynamic Spatio-Temporal Adapter (DST-Adapter) to adaptively regulate the fusion of different feature pathways and enhance spatio-temporal representations according to the input content. This work provides a more flexible and efficient modeling paradigm for 4D point cloud video understanding.
