details:
  zh: >-
    近日，实验室作为合作单位参与的深圳市科技创新局重点产业研发项目 “面向自主式交通指挥与管控的多模态具身智能关键技术研发”
    正式获批立项，实验室负责人周逊教授担任项目高校负责人。


    项目面向城市交通指挥与管控的智能化、自主化发展需求，重点开展多模态具身智能相关关键技术研究，探索人工智能与交通感知、决策及指挥管控的深度融合，为构建更加智能、高效的自主式交通管理系统提供技术支撑。


    实验室将围绕项目目标，与合作单位共同推进关键技术研发与应用验证，助力智能交通技术创新与实际应用。
  en: >-
    Recently, the project “Research and Development of Key Multimodal Embodied
    Intelligence Technologies for Autonomous Traffic Command and Control”, with
    our lab serving as a collaborating institution, was officially approved
    under the Key Industrial R&D Program of the Shenzhen Science, Technology and
    Innovation Bureau. Professor Xun Zhou, head of our lab, serves as the
    university lead for the project.


    The project addresses the growing demand for intelligent and autonomous urban traffic command and control. It focuses on key technologies in multimodal embodied intelligence and explores the integration of artificial intelligence with traffic perception, decision-making, and command-and-control systems.


    Our lab will work closely with the project partners to advance key technology development and application validation, contributing to the development of more intelligent and efficient autonomous traffic management systems.
