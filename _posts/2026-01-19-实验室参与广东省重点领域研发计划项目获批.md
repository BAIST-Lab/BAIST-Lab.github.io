  zh: |-
    近日，实验室参与的广东省重点领域研发计划项目 “面向车路云一体化的高性能通感算关键技术与设备研发” 正式获批立项。

    项目面向车路云一体化发展需求，围绕高性能通信、感知与计算等关键技术开展研究，并推进相关设备研发，为智能交通系统中车、路、云多主体的高效协同提供技术支撑。
  en: >-
    Recently, the project “Research and Development of Key Technologies and
    Equipment for High-Performance Integrated Communication, Sensing, and
    Computing in Vehicle-Road-Cloud Integration”, with participation from our
    lab, was officially approved under the Guangdong Provincial Key-Area R&D
    Program.


    The project targets the development needs of vehicle-road-cloud integration and focuses on key technologies in high-performance communication, sensing, and computing, together with related equipment development. It aims to support efficient coordination among vehicles, roadside infrastructure, and cloud platforms in intelligent transportation systems.
