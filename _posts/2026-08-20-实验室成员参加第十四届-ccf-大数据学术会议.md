  zh: >-
    近日，周逊教授、黄锐和鲍侃宇参加第十四届 CCF 大数据学术会议（CCF BigData）。


    会议期间，周逊教授作题为 “面向跨场景异质性城市大数据的时空基座模型研究” 的学术报告。报告聚焦复杂城市环境中不同场景之间存在的数据分布差异与空间异质性问题，介绍了实验室在城市时空基座模型方面的近期研究进展，并探讨了如何提升模型在不同城市、不同区域及多样化应用场景中的泛化与适应能力。


    黄锐同学和鲍侃宇同学在会议 Poster 环节展示了实验室近期的两项研究成果。
  en: >-
    Recently, Professor Xun Zhou, Rui Huang, and Kanyu Bao attended the 14th CCF
    Conference on Big Data (CCF BigData)。


    During the conference, Professor Xun Zhou delivered an academic talk titled “Spatiotemporal Foundation Models for Cross-Scenario Heterogeneous Urban Big Data.” The talk focused on data distribution differences and spatial heterogeneity across complex urban scenarios, introduced the lab’s recent progress in urban spatiotemporal foundation models, and discussed how to improve model generalization and adaptability across different cities, regions, and application scenarios.


    In addition, Rui Huang and Kanyu Bao presented two recent research works from the lab during the poster sessions.
