  zh: >-
    近日，实验室两项研究成果被 The Fourteenth International Conference on Learning
    Representations（ICLR 2026）
    接收。相关研究聚焦人工智能在复杂现实场景中的泛化与安全应用问题，分别探索了多模态空间—信号基础模型在跨场景无线定位中的应用，以及基于安全多方计算的大语言模型隐私保护高效调优方法。


    论文 “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” 由 Yong Chu、Xun Zhou、Zenglin Xu、Hui Wang 和 Yue Yu 共同完成。


    无线定位是自动驾驶、扩展现实和智能制造等 5G/6G 应用的重要基础能力。然而，无线信号容易受到环境变化影响，现有数据驱动方法通常依赖大量标注数据，在面对新的环境和场景时泛化能力有限。


    针对这一问题，该研究提出多模态基础模型 SigMap。模型首先设计了周期自适应掩码策略（cycle-adaptive masking strategy），根据无线信道的周期性特征动态调整掩码模式，从而学习更加鲁棒的无线信号表征。在此基础上，研究进一步提出 “Map-as-Prompt” 框架，将三维地理信息以轻量级软提示的形式引入模型，使模型能够利用空间环境信息实现高效的跨场景适应。


    实验结果表明，SigMap 在多项无线定位任务上取得了领先性能，并在未见环境中展现出较强的零样本泛化能力，显著优于多种监督学习和自监督学习基线。该工作为融合无线信号与地理空间信息、构建能够适应不同环境的无线感知基础模型提供了新的研究路径。


    论文 “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC” 由 Jinglong Luo、Zhuo Zhang、Yehong Zhang、Shiyu Liu、Ye Dong、Hui Wang、Yue Yu、Xun Zhou 和 Zenglin Xu 共同完成。


    大语言模型在众多领域展现出强大的能力，但在医疗、金融等隐私敏感场景中，严格的数据隐私要求限制了训练数据的获取与使用。安全多方计算（Secure Multi-party Computation，MPC）能够为数据和模型参数提供理论上的隐私保护，但现有基于 MPC 的大模型研究主要集中于推理阶段。若进一步进行模型微调，反向传播、优化器以及自注意力等操作会带来巨大的计算与通信开销。


    针对这一问题，该研究提出 SecP-Tuning，一个面向大语言模型的高效 MPC 隐私保护提示调优框架。SecP-Tuning 将 Forward-only Tuning（FoT） 引入“数据所有者—服务器交互”范式，从而避免在反向传播和优化过程中执行复杂的隐私保护计算。同时，研究设计了高效的隐私保护随机特征注意力（Random Feature Attention，RFA），降低传统 Softmax 自注意力的计算复杂度，并规避部分难以在 MPC 环境中高效实现的非线性运算。


    实验结果显示，相比全参数监督微调和基于梯度的提示调优，SecP-Tuning 分别实现约 12 倍和 16 倍的端到端加速，通信开销分别降低约 18 倍和 20 倍。在五项少样本任务中，其平均性能达到 82.45，高于全参数监督微调的 79.90 和传统提示调优的 73.73。此外，其“黑盒/API式”的隐私保护调优方式避免了梯度或模型参数传输可能带来的内存泄露风险，在效率、性能、部署便利性与隐私保护之间实现了较好的平衡。
  en: >-
    Recently, two research papers from our lab were accepted by The Fourteenth
    International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2026). The two
    studies focus on the generalization and secure deployment of artificial
    intelligence in complex real-world scenarios, exploring multi-modal
    spatial-signal foundation models for cross-scenario wireless localization
    and efficient privacy-preserving tuning of large language models based on
    Secure Multi-party Computation (MPC), respectively.


    The paper “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” was authored by Yong Chu, Xun Zhou, Zenglin Xu, Hui Wang, and Yue Yu.


    Wireless localization is a critical enabling technology for emerging 5G/6G applications such as autonomous driving, extended reality, and smart manufacturing. However, wireless signals are highly sensitive to environmental changes, while existing data-driven methods often rely heavily on large amounts of labeled data and exhibit limited generalization when applied to new environments and scenarios.


    To address these challenges, the study proposes SigMap, a multi-modal foundation model for wireless localization. First, SigMap introduces a cycle-adaptive masking strategy that dynamically adjusts masking patterns according to the periodic characteristics of wireless channels, enabling the model to learn more robust wireless representations. Building upon this, the study further proposes a “Map-as-Prompt” framework that incorporates 3D geographic information into the model through lightweight soft prompts, allowing spatial environmental information to facilitate efficient cross-scenario adaptation.


    Experimental results show that SigMap achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple wireless localization tasks and demonstrates strong zero-shot generalization in unseen environments, significantly outperforming a range of supervised and self-supervised baselines. This work provides a new direction for integrating wireless signals with geographic information and developing wireless sensing foundation models capable of adapting to diverse environments.


    The paper “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC” was authored by Jinglong Luo, Zhuo Zhang, Yehong Zhang, Shiyu Liu, Ye Dong, Hui Wang, Yue Yu, Xun Zhou, and Zenglin Xu.


    Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated powerful capabilities across a wide range of fields. However, in privacy-sensitive domains such as healthcare and finance, stringent privacy requirements often restrict the accessibility and use of training data. Secure Multi-party Computation (MPC) provides theoretical privacy guarantees for both data and model parameters, but existing MPC-based research on LLMs has primarily focused on inference. Extending MPC to model tuning introduces substantial computational and communication overhead, particularly in backward propagation, optimization, and self-attention operations.


    To address these challenges, the study proposes SecP-Tuning, an efficient MPC-based privacy-preserving prompt tuning framework for LLMs. SecP-Tuning integrates Forward-only Tuning (FoT) into a “data owner–server interaction” paradigm, eliminating the need for expensive privacy-preserving computations during backward propagation and optimization. In addition, the study develops an efficient privacy-preserving Random Feature Attention (RFA) mechanism to reduce the computational complexity of conventional Softmax-based self-attention while avoiding nonlinear operations that are difficult to implement efficiently under MPC.


    Experimental results demonstrate that, compared with full-parameter Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and gradient-based prompt tuning, SecP-Tuning achieves approximately 12× and 16× end-to-end speedups, respectively, while reducing communication overhead by approximately 18× and 20×. Across five few-shot tasks, SecP-Tuning achieves an average performance score of 82.45, outperforming SFT at 79.90 and conventional prompt tuning at 73.73. Moreover, its “black-box/API-style” privacy-preserving tuning paradigm avoids potential memory leakage risks associated with the transmission of gradients or model parameters, achieving a strong balance among efficiency, performance, deployability, and privacy preservation.
