Yuanpeng Yao received his B.S. in Electronic Information Science and Technology from Nankai University in 2023 and his M.S. in Communication and Information Systems from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2026. His current research interests include vector databases, KV cache reuse and cache management, and inference-aware vector retrieval and cache co-optimization.
