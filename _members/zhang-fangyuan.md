    张芳源，哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）计算机科学与技术学院教授、博士生导师，国家级高层次青年人才。本科毕业于上海交通大学ACM试点班（致远荣誉计划），博士毕业于香港中文大学，此前在华为香港研究所担任研究员，并曾在微软亚洲研究院、腾讯、阿里云进行研究实习，长期致力于高效大数据管理与分析领域的研究，方向涵盖大数据计算的算法设计与分析、大数据挖掘，以及AI与数据管理（Data+AI）的融合技术。近年来在SIGMOD、VLDB、KDD、ICSE、TKDE等顶级会议和期刊上发表论文20余篇，多项研究成果已在知名互联网公司的核心产品中落地部署。张芳源教授担任SIGKDD、ICDE、WSDM、TKDE等多个CCF推荐A/B类会议和期刊的审稿人，并曾获评SIGKDD Outstanding Reviewer。此外，他也是香港青年科学家协会会员，曾担任香港中文大学（深圳）业界导师。
  en: |-
    Zhang Fangyuan is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology of Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen), and a national-level high-level young talent. He graduated from the ACM Pilot Program (Zhiyuan Honor Program) of Shanghai Jiao Tong University for his undergraduate studies, and obtained his doctoral degree from the Chinese University of Hong Kong. Previously, he worked as a researcher at Huawei Hong Kong Research Institute and conducted research internships at Microsoft Asia Research Institute, Tencent, and Alibaba Cloud. He has been dedicated to research in the field of efficient big data management and analysis, covering algorithm design and analysis for big data computing, big data mining, and the integration technology of AI and data management (Data + AI). In recent years, he has published over 20 papers in top conferences and journals such as SIGMOD, VLDB, KDD, ICSE, and TKDE. Many of his research results have been deployed in the core products of well-known Internet companies. Professor Zhang Fangyuan serves as a reviewer for several CCF-recommended A/B category conferences and journals, including SIGKDD, ICDE, WSDM, and TKDE, and has been awarded the SIGKDD Outstanding Reviewer title. Additionally, he is a member of the Hong Kong Young Scientists Association and has served as an industry mentor at the University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen).
