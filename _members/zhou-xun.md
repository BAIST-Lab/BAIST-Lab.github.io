周逊教授2014毕业于美国明尼苏达大学，获得计算机科学博士学位。后在美国爱荷华大学任教并晋升终身副教授。曾任爱荷华大学Tippie商学院商业分析(Business Analytics)系博士生项目主任，Henry B. Tippie 特聘教授，并兼任应用数学与计算科学、信息科学博士导师。2023年秋季起任哈工大深圳计算机科学与技术学院教授，博士生导师。IEEE高级会员。入选国家级青年人才项目（海外）。
{: .lang-zh}

Prof. Xun Zhou received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota in 2014. He was previously a tenured Associate Professor at the University of Iowa, served as Director of the Business Analytics Ph.D. program at Tippie College, was a Henry B. Tippie Distinguished Professor, and also a Ph.D. advisor in Applied Mathematics, Computational Science, and Informatics. Since Fall 2023, he has been a Professor and PhD advisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, HIT Shenzhen. IEEE Senior Member. Selected for the national young talent program (overseas).
